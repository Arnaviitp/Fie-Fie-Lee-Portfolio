About

Fei-Fei Li is a Chinese-American computer scientist, author, and AI researcher. She is widely recognized as one of the most influential figures in Artificial Intelligence, specializing in computer vision, machine learning, and cognitive neuroscience. She advocates for human-centered AI and inclusive technology that benefits society.

Early Life

Fei-Fei Li was born in Beijing, China, in 1976. At the age of 16, she immigrated with her family to the United States, where they initially struggled financially. Despite these challenges, she excelled academically while working in her family’s dry-cleaning business. Her early experiences shaped her perspective on resilience, opportunity, and the social impact of technology.

ImageNet Project

Dr. Li is best known for leading the groundbreaking ImageNet project, a large-scale visual database that helped train deep learning algorithms to recognize objects in images. ImageNet was the catalyst for the modern deep learning revolution and transformed computer vision research globally.

Research & Contributions

  • Large-scale image recognition and visual understanding.
  • Neuroscience-inspired machine learning, linking human cognition with AI.
  • AI applications in healthcare, robotics, and education for social good.
  • Promoting ethical, transparent, and inclusive AI development through human-centered design.

Media & Outreach

Dr. Li is committed to broadening participation in AI. She co-founded AI4ALL, a nonprofit dedicated to increasing diversity and inclusion in AI education. She is also an influential voice in policy, advising governments and organizations on AI ethics and governance. Her TED Talk on teaching computers to understand pictures has been viewed millions of times worldwide.
